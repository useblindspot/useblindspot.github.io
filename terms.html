Terms

Blindspot is not financial advice. It does not tell you what to buy, sell, or hold, and it has no opinion on any decision you describe to it. It is a reflection prompt. Any decision you make is yours.

What this is

Blindspot is a free tool that reads a decision you describe in plain language and reflects it back against six documented cognitive biases. The intent is to make you articulate your reasoning before you act, because articulating it is usually where the weak part becomes visible.

What this is not

  • Not a validated instrument. There is no validation study, no reliability testing, and no comparison against any established psychological measure. The scores are indicative, not diagnostic.
  • Not financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to take or avoid any position.
  • Not a psychological assessment. A low or high score says nothing clinical about you.
  • Not a predictor of outcomes. Using Blindspot will not make your decisions profitable. No claim is made that it improves results in any way.

Accuracy

Blindspot works by matching linguistic patterns. It will miss biases that are present, and it will flag biases that are not. Treat a result as a question worth asking yourself, never as a finding.
